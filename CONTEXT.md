# Domain Context — ka4-today-bot

## Glossary

- client — the person receiving coaching through the Telegram bot, a coach's tracked trainee, identified by their Telegram chat and a linked client record; the actor who logs measurements, follows a prescribed workout, and records the workouts they actually performed. NOT "coach" — the coach is the person managing clients and prescribing plans through the admin API, not the one training.
